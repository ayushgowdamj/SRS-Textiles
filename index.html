Our Story

Crafting Silk Excellence Since Decades

Sri Ranganatha Textile Silk Mills (SRTSM) is a trusted name in raw mulberry silk manufacturing, rooted in the heart of India's finest sericulture regions. Founded on the principles of quality, integrity, and consistent supply, we have built a legacy of delivering premium-grade raw silk to dealers and bulk buyers across the country.

Our manufacturing facility combines time-honored silk reeling traditions with modern quality control systems, ensuring every batch of silk meets the highest industry standards. From cocoon procurement to final yarn delivery, we maintain rigorous oversight at every step.

We believe in building long-term partnerships with our dealers and buyers, offering reliable supply schedules, competitive quality, and a team that stands behind every transaction.

Premium Silk Products

Premium Grade

Raw Silk Cocoons

Fresh, high-filament-length mulberry silk cocoons sourced directly from expert sericulture farms. Available in white and yellow varieties with consistent quality throughout.

Specifications
  • Filament Length: 800–1400 m/cocoon
  • Reelability: 80–90%
  • Neatness: Grade A & B
  • Available in Bulk Quantities

Raw Silk Yarn & Thread

Reeled raw silk yarn of exceptional uniformity and tensile strength. Suitable for power looms, handlooms, and high-end textile weaving operations. Supplied in bobbins and skeins.

Specifications
  • Denier Range: 20/22D to 100D

  • Moisture Regain: ≤11%
